# docs/ index

Documentation of the *Mobile Trainer (Japan)* disassembly.  The source is the hand-maintained RGBDS tree at the repository root (`README.md`, `STYLE.md`, `INSTALL.md`); the top-level status is in
[`../REVERSE_ENGINEERING.md`](../REVERSE_ENGINEERING.md).  This directory holds the reference documents and the research notes that carry the evidence behind the statuses (`CONFIRMED` / `PROBABLE` / `HYPOTHESIS`)
written in the source comments.

Evidence lives in these places, from the source outwards:

| where | what |
|---|---|
| `[STATUS]` comments and `; ---- <kind> ... [STATUS] note` headers in the `.asm` files, `ram/*.asm`, `consts.asm` | the last state of every claim, with a short note |
| `docs/research/*.md` (this directory) | the reasoning: method, addresses, retractions, open questions.  **New findings are recorded here and at the code.** |
| `config/symbols/*.tsv`, `config/regions/*.tsv`, `config/ram/*.tsv`, `config/xrefs.tsv` | frozen per-symbol / per-region evidence text (bootstrap tables, `config/README.md`) |
| `analysis/`, `traces/` | machine-readable analysis output and emulator coverage (dynamic evidence) |

## Status of the documents

* **reference (current)**: still describes the ROM or the source as it is.
* **historical**: written while the source was one generated `src/bankNN.asm` per bank (or as output of an analysis tool over the ROM).  The findings and the addresses (`bank:addr`) are still the evidence;
  only the *names* changed: generic `Label_BB_AAAA` labels now appear as local labels `.lAAAA` (or `.loop`/`.done`/`.skip`) inside their function, other neutral names are unchanged, and semantic names adopted
  from the notes are in the source.  Each of these files has a note at the top; find code by address with `grep -rn '; BB:AAAA' --include=*.asm .` or in `build/mobile_trainer.sym`.
  Commands of the frozen bootstrap pipeline in them (`make regen`, `make verify`, `make tree*`, `tools/gen_asm.py ...`) are the record of how the work was done, not instructions for the current tree
  (`make regen`, `verify`, `tree*` refuse to run; `make legacy-check` runs the generator in a temp dir).

## Later passes (added after the freeze)

* `audio_format.md` - sound stream/song format and what the commands, instruments and tables do (reference, current); re-checked on 2026-10-05 by two independent verifiers.
* `audio2_verify_static.md` (code and data read, no emulation; scripts in `../analysis/audio2_verify/`) and `audio2_verify_dynamic.md` (the ROM's own driver on mGBA with the real songs and synthetic streams; harness `../tools/trace/apu_probe.c`, scripts `../tools/trace/audio2_*.py`,
  results and `reproduce.sh` in `../analysis/audio2_verify_dyn/`) - adversarial verification of the audio pass 2 and its names; the corrections they found are applied in `audio_format.md` (section 12).
* `naming2_ram2.md`, `naming2_data2.md` - RAM and data-label naming passes; `naming2_verify_ram2.md`, `naming2_verify_data2a.md`, `naming2_verify_data2b.md` - their adversarial verification (status corrections live there).
* `naming2_fn4.md` - naming pass 4: the residual neutral `Function_*` labels (30 named, 29 left neutral with the reasons); `naming2_verify_fn4.md` - its independent verification (the corrections are merged into `analysis/naming2/fn4_renames.tsv`).
* `naming2_ram3.md` - RAM and operand pass 3: the banked `wKbdSlideDeltaRow` ($D725), the bank-1 SRAM variable page `sVarPage` ... `sKbdInputMode`, 34 raw pointer operands replaced by labels (record: `analysis/naming2/rawptr1_sites.tsv`), and (section 5) the 258 screen-local aliases of the overlay variables in `ram/overlays.asm` (manifest: `analysis/naming2/overlay_aliases.tsv`); `naming2_verify_ram3.md` - their independent verification by six readers (276 rows: 229 upheld, 42 corrected, 4 narrowed, 1 widened; the correction rows are `analysis/naming2/verify_overlay_fixes.tsv`).
* `naming2_ramop11.md` - the 123 neutral banked uses of the mail library and the sound driver written as names (138 rows of `ramop11_manual.tsv`), the decoder sites of `$D023` that the old name did not fit, the three new names (`wMailReplyRecord`, `wSoundDrv_VibratoTri`, `wSoundDrv_ParamDirtyMask`); `naming2_verify_ramop11.md` - its independent verification (every row read, L5 re-derived for the library, 14 rows corrected).
* `naming2_audio6.md` - structural names for the 316 neutral labels of the song and effect streams (`_Loop`, `_AfterJump`, `_SubN`, `TrackPtrs`), why 213 are CONFIRMED and 103 PROBABLE, what the names do not claim; `naming2_verify_audio6.md` - its independent verification (a ROM-only decoder: 316 of 316; the status correction from the natural traces).
* `naming2_romop2.md` - the second pass over the ROM pointers: palette reads, HDMA tile windows and tilemap Ptr reads inside blocks as `Label + $offset` (with the address in a comment), 16 string pointers and a palette as new neutral labels in front of the data, `dw CopyBytes`; the rules (a read of known length whose every line is data of that kind), what stays numeric; `naming2_verify_romop2.md` - its independent verification (the real ROM code of 76 sites replayed, the tool attacked and hardened).
* `naming2_pad1.md` - the three joypad variables hold one bit per button (CONFIRMED three ways) and 306 tests that follow a read of one are written as `PADB_*` / `PADF_*`; the hidden-mode combination B + Select + Right, the five injections into `hJoyPressed`, what stays numeric and why; `naming2_verify_pad1.md` - its independent verification (every rewrite read, ROM bytes decoded, 136 sites checked frame by frame, tool defects and mutation testing).
* `naming2_sfx1.md` - the macro `play_sfx` for the 367 eight-line sound calls of the user interface and 20 PROBABLE constants `SFX_*` named by the role of the call (343 sites; ten ids stay numbers and why), what the idiom does to A, BC, DE, HL and the WRAM bank; `naming2_verify_sfx1.md` - its independent verification (an executor and two readers, every site read).
* `naming2_romop1.md` - ROM pointer operands (`ld hl|de|bc, $XXXX` with an address below `$8000`) written as labels where a consumer rule proves the routine reads the register as a ROM pointer (708 operands, 1,367 bank loads as `BANK(label)`, 161 new labels `<Table>_Entry<N>`; 23 rules in `analysis/naming2/rom_consumers.tsv`, tool `tools/apply_rom_operands.py`), the corrected entry index of the sprite object tables (`id & $3F`), the classes of what stays numeric; `naming2_verify_romop1.md` - its independent verification (four readers: tool and rules, `Sprite_InitSlot` sites with a replay of 21 scenarios, the other consumers, documents and rebuild).
* `naming2_ramop8.md` - the two screen buffers of WRAM bank 7 (`wScreenTileMap`, `wScreenAttrMap`) written as names (303 operands; rules with the bank in A, effect `keeps if A=0`), `naming2_verify_ramop8.md` - its independent verification.
* `naming2_ramop9.md` - the tile staging windows of WRAM banks 2 and 3, the account work area of bank 3, the dialog tile stage of bank 7 and the palette fade buffers (20 names; 941 pointer operands, 104 neutral uses and 12 table words written as names; proofs `dest`, bank `*`, replays for `switch` rows, `(direct)`, `CAVEAT`, by-hand rows), `naming2_verify_ramop9.md` - its two independent verifications.
* `naming2_ramop10.md` - the mail library (bank 0F, WRAM bank 5), the sound driver (bank 04, WRAM bank 1), the mail screens' buffers and the browser and HTML work areas of banks 4, 5 and 6 (31 names; 494 pointer operands, 197 uses of old or neutral names and 12 table words written as names; proofs: the bank at the consumer, `(direct)` behind a switch and a loop head with `overrun`, the label join, `needs fixed` for the library invariant, A = 0 only for `keeps if A=0`, `; raw` never rewritten), `naming2_verify_ramop10.md` - its four independent verifications (the tool and the rules, the library and the sound driver, the mail screens, the browser).
* `naming2_ramop7.md` - banked WRAM pointer operands (`$D000-$DFFF`) written as names where a consumer rule proves the bank: the 14 sprite slots and the palette buffer of bank 7 (1,535 operands, 612 `wSpriteSlots + N` expressions; records `analysis/naming2/wramx_consumers.tsv`, `wramx_calls.tsv`, `ramop7_manual.tsv`, tool `tools/apply_ram_operands.py --areas wramx`, `tools/line_addresses.py`), the four generator names that ran under bank 1; `naming2_verify_ramop7.md` - its independent verification.
* `naming2_ramop6.md` - RAM pointer operands of WRAM0, HRAM and the hardware registers written as names (1,394 operands, record: `analysis/naming2/ramop6_names.tsv`, tool `tools/apply_ram_operands.py`), what the first version got wrong; `naming2_verify_ramop6.md` - its independent verification.
* `naming2_label5.md` - naming pass 5: the neutral `Label_` jump targets (410 named, 7 left neutral; record: `analysis/naming2/label5_renames_pkg*.tsv`), the mistyped tables and dead fragments it found, the comm scene sprite renames; `naming2_verify_label5.md` - its independent verification (425 rows, the corrections are merged into the manifests).
* `naming2_sram4.md` - SRAM names pass 4: 85 bank-qualified SRAM names and 349 raw operands replaced (record: `analysis/naming2/sram4_names.tsv`, `sram4_sites.tsv`), the overlap rule for banked names; `naming2_verify_sram4.md` - their independent verification by three readers (names 60 upheld, 25 corrected; sites 186 upheld, 3 corrected).
