Findings that matter beyond the names (the details are in the package sections):

* **`FFE0` is an index into the link pointer table**, not "+$E link heap position" (`naming_g8.md`; the ram2 idea row `hHtmlLinkHeapOffset`): `Browser_PageView_FollowLink` reads `$D600 + 2 * [record+$E]`.

* **`C286` is a one-shot sound latch**, not an "API poll counter"; **`C10E/C10F` in `Tilemap_FillRectSequential` are a constant and a start tile**, not a counter; the title idle counter is **big-endian**.
* **`Html_Layout_ClearAllFloats` may never terminate** when a page ends inside an unclosed list (the retry at `74:5293` ran 292,374 times in one natural scenario): HYPOTHESIS, recorded as an open question.
* Original-program quirks and stale source text found on the way: the swapped constants of the second field of the phone slot menu (`67:5017-501D`), nine spurious `Label_68_5B6C..5B8C` labels from a mistyped
