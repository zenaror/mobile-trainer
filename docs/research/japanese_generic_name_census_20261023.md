# Japanese generic-name census and screen catalog IDs (2026-10-23)

This bounded, independent census checked the Japanese maintained ASM/INC roots and GFX catalog outside the separately audited bank 41–47 graphic owners. It did not read the paused English overlay or the Operator ZIP. The source snapshot is commit 0fa24aa195311a3f84106f816ffae896ad6f8d83; exact counts are scoped to the files in that private snapshot.

## Assembly names

The snapshot contains 345 ASM/INC files outside protected sample data. Removing the eight graphic-owner ASM files for banks 41–47 leaves 337: 325 across the six maintained roots, seven under constants, and five under ram.

An anchored name-pattern census found 13 exact address-form Data_BB_AAAA definitions and zero definitions for the searched Function, Func, Sub, Unknown, Code, Label, String, Font, Tiles, Tilemap, Attrmap, Palette and Table prefixes. The 13 Data names match the reviewed disposition in generic_data_name_followup_20261023.md and remain neutral: the bounded snapshot found no symbolic references beyond their definitions or exact literal address operands, but this negative search does not exclude computed access. Their presence and locations are statically confirmed; a more specific content or entry interpretation remains unproved.

The narrow pattern does not cover every resource whose interpretation is still partial. Six deliberately qualified names were identified as side coverage:

- UnresolvedTiles_7F_7830
- CandidateTiles_51_5EE1
- CandidateTiles_26_7840
- CandidatePalette_26_73E0
- CandidatePalette_29_5376
- CandidateMapData_23_7C03

These names preserve uncertainty and bank/address context. The census does not promote their purpose or resource classes.

## Graphics paths and catalog

The checked non-frame GFX paths contain no directory exactly named bankNN and no strict physical stem of the forms tiles_XXXX, tilemap_XXXX, attrmap_XXXX, palette_XXXX, font_XXXX, data_XXXX, string_XXXX or table_XXXX. A broader hexadecimal-suffix search produces false positives because many current filenames include meaningful resource, owner, VRAM, slice or candidate context.

Two physical asset stems remain only type-candidate plus address: candidate_tiles_26_7840 (.2bpp and .png) and candidate_palette_26_73e0 (.pal). Their neutral names remain appropriate. Their catalogued formats are PROBABLE heuristics, not proof of a reader or product purpose.

The independent census found 15 stale screen identifiers ending in tilemap_AAAA in the name column of gfx/screens.tsv. These were not physical paths: their PNG, tilemap and attrmap columns already pointed to descriptive, existing files. The table is generated by tools/screen_png.py derive and explicitly must not be edited by hand. Running that generator in a private copy and then in the active checkout changed exactly those 15 IDs, with the same output SHA-256; no other row fields or records changed. The exact prior-to-current mapping and physical tilemap hashes are in screen_catalog_id_mapping_20261023.tsv.

## Independent review and limits

A second read-only reviewer independently accepted the 337-file scope, the 13 Data definitions, the two candidate GFX stems and all 15 screen-ID matches. The reviewer confirmed the ID fields were not file paths and that all three physical path columns existed. It also confirmed that the exact-name census does not settle six separately qualified Candidate/Unresolved labels.

This is a bounded lexical/static census, not proof that every code function or data block is semantically understood, that indirect access is absent, or that any asset is unused. The independent bank 41–47 frame/resource audit is documented separately.
