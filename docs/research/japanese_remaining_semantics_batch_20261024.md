### Conditional geometry contract for the unreferenced mask-rectangle copy (2026-10-24)

The source at `48:4A8C` is byte-identical to `00:091C`, but byte identity does
not establish an entry or caller for the bank-48 copy. Both implementations
process `B` rows of `C` bytes with `[HL]=([HL]&D)|E`; after a row, they add
`(-C)&31` to the pointer already advanced by the inner loop. For `C=1..255`,
the resulting next-row advance is `ceil(C/32)*32`. `C=0` decrements through
256 cells, adds no row correction, and advances 256; `B=0` wraps to 256 rows.
Thus “32-byte row stride” requires `C=1..32`, a producer contract not
established for `48:4A8C`. `BankSwitch_H` selects a region from `H`; ROM
addresses (`H<$80`) can remap the executing ROMX bank, while a writable
non-ROM destination and stable stack/mapping state are required for the helper
to continue and store safely. Its natural producer, intended use and normal
input domain remain UNKNOWN/HYPOTHESIS. The arithmetic is a static derivation
from the two instruction sequences, not runtime evidence.
